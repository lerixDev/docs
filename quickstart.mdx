## 1. Create an account and a project

<a href="https://app.lerix.dev">Create an account</a> on the Lerix dashboard, then create a project. In **Project Settings** you'll find the two values every SDK needs:

- **API key** (`apiKey`)
- **Project ID** (`projectId`)

## 2. Pick your platform

## 3. Confirm it works

After initialising, trigger a test error. It appears on the project's **Issues** page within seconds, with the device, OS and app version attached. The [Error Tracking](/modules/error-tracking) guide covers grouping, severity and the AI assistant.
